About

Breakfast and lunch

Steve's Diner on Portsmouth Avenue serves breakfast and lunch: a whole lineup of eggs Benedict (including the California Benedict), omelets, pancakes and Belgian waffles, plus lunch plates like the gyro plate.

Gluten-free bread is available.
