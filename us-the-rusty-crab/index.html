About

Family seafood & more, since 2011

The Rusty Crab has been serving seafood and more in Mars Hill since 2011: lobster rolls, fried whole clams, scallops and grilled haddock, plus maple steak tips for the turf side of the table.

There's a small bar for cocktails, and outdoor seating.
