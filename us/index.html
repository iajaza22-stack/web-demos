Questions

FAQ

Do I pay anything for the demo?

No. The demo is free and there's no obligation.

When do I pay?

Only after you've reviewed and approved the finished site. We then send you a secure payment link for $350.

Is the domain included?

We help you choose and connect your own domain (like yourbusiness.com). The domain fee itself is paid directly to the registrar, usually around $10 to $20 a year.

What do you need from me?

For the demo, nothing beyond what's already public. For the finished site, a few photos, your menu or list of services, and any changes you'd like.
